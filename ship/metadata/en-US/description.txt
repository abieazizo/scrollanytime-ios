You were going to scroll anyway. Make it Torah.

Scroll Anytime is an endless feed of short, real Torah lessons — one swipe at a time, from the rabbis you choose. No accounts required, no noise, no distractions. Open the app and Torah is already playing.

CHOOSE YOUR RABBIS
Pick the voices you love and the feed becomes theirs. Every clip carries the rabbi's name — real Torah from real rabbis, shared with permission.

IT LEARNS WHAT YOU LOVE
The more you watch, the better it gets. Finish a clip, save it, follow a rabbi — the feed quietly learns and brings you more of what moves you. It never runs out.

READ ALONG
On clips that have them, tap CC and the words light up as the rabbi says them, gold on the current word — and you can read the whole transcript on its own, tapping any line to jump there. More of the library gains captions every week.

FOLLOW, SEARCH, COLLECT
A Following feed of just your rabbis. Search by rabbi, by topic, and inside the transcripts we have. Save clips into your own collections — Shalom bayit, Emunah for hard weeks, whatever you're building.

YOUR WEEK OF TORAH
A daily seder goal with a gentle streak (one shmita day of grace each week), a weekly recap, and beautiful share cards for the moments worth sending.

SAVE WHAT MOVES YOU
Double-tap any clip to keep it in My Torah — your personal library of the moments worth returning to. Your saved clips even play offline.

BUILD A DAILY SEDER
Set a daily learning goal — ten minutes, twenty, or no limit. Watch your streak grow day by day, with a calendar of every day you learned. Miss a day once in a week? A Shmita day keeps your streak whole.

WORKS OFFLINE
Your saved Torah and your latest clips stay with you, connection or not.
