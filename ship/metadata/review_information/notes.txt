Scroll Anytime is an endless feed of short Torah video lessons (vertical, swipe-up format) from
rabbis and Torah organizations. It is a content/education app: watch, save, follow rabbis, set a
daily learning goal, and track a learning streak.

WORKS WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
The entire app is usable with no sign-in: launch it and the feed plays immediately. Sign-in is
optional and exists solely so saves, follows and streaks survive a new device.

Sign-in by email one-time code (no password) is always available, and is what the review account
below uses. Sign in with Apple is listed FIRST wherever a provider button appears. A provider whose
credentials are not configured is NOT SHOWN AT ALL in this build — there is no button in the app
that reports an unavailable feature.

Account deletion is available in-app: Profile → Delete account (two confirmations, permanently
removes all user data across every table, effective immediately).

CONTENT RIGHTS
The app publishes short excerpts of Torah lectures. Permission was given to us directly and in
person by the rabbis who teach them; it was given VERBALLY, so there are no signed licence
documents and this file must never claim otherwise. Lessons come from the rabbis' own public social
accounts and from iTorah. Every clip is attributed on-screen, Profile > About & Credits names every
contributor with a contact address, and any lesson is removed immediately on request from the
teacher, their family or their publisher. Nothing in the app is monetized.

LOCATION
The app contains no location code of any kind and never requests location permission.

PURCHASES
There are no purchases, subscriptions, or external purchase links in this version. (A "Sponsor"
page exists on the website only; it is not present in the app.)

PUSH NOTIFICATIONS
The app asks for notification permission ONCE, and only after the person has watched enough
for a day to count — never on first open, never among the first tips. The ask is a card at the end
of that clip: "Want a reminder tomorrow?" with "Remind me at 8:00 PM" and "Not now". Only the
gold button raises the system prompt. "Not now" is offered at most once more, seven days later,
and then never again.

Reminders themselves are LOCAL notifications the app schedules on the device: one a day at the
time the person picks, suppressed during quiet hours and never on Shabbat or Yom Tov. Remote
pushes (a milestone, the weekly recap) are sent from our own server through APNs; there is no
third-party push or marketing SDK. Everything can be turned off in Profile → Reminders, and if
permission was denied the app links straight to Settings instead of asking again.

OFFLINE
The app works offline: saved clips are listed and recently watched clips play with no connection
(try airplane mode after watching a few clips). On a FIRST launch with no connection the app shows
a branded offline screen with a Retry button — never a blank page.

CAPTIONS
Clips that have word-level captions show them on the CC control, with the current word highlighted,
and offer a full readable transcript. Caption coverage is partial today and growing as the library
is processed, so some clips have no CC control. The store description says exactly this.
